# Windows playtest recording, 2026-11-18

The user played the private Windows release test `c9e73d2` for 91 minutes on
their own PC (Windows 11, Ryzen 9 9950X, RTX 5070 Ti, 2560×1440) and supplied
the recording, `Player.log` and the final save. The colony is the Spaced Out!
save carried since 0.1.4; the session ran from cycle 11 to cycle 23 and from
three duplicants to five. The user reported that pipes were not passing their
full rate and asked for anything else the recording shows. The recording,
log and save stay private.

## Pipes passed half their rate behind every machine

The plumbing overlay in the recording shows a full pipe from the liquid pump
to the first reservoir and, behind that reservoir, a 10 kg packet in every
other cell. The same pattern runs through the reservoir chain, along the
pipe to the metal refinery and through the refinery's coolant loop.

Cause: a machine outlet published the packet it added as mass received
during the tick, which ONI may not move until the tick after next. ONI's own
outlets consolidate the packet at once. Every machine outlet therefore
passed a packet every other tick. Pumps, valves and filters were not
affected ([throughput](mixed-pipes.md#throughput)). Fixed: outlets and the
two pipe bridges consolidate as the game does. This is managed code only;
solver arithmetic, checkpoint bytes and the save format are unchanged, and
a colony saved by `c9e73d2` loads as before.

The new in-game check `pipe-throughput` reproduces the recording on the
playtest build (a reservoir passes 250.0 kg in 50 seconds, its pipe holding
10, 0, 10, 0, 10, 0 kg by cell) and passes with the fix (500.0 kg, every
cell full). It runs in the acceptance batches from now on.

## Readings

- The selected pipe's details printed its temperature a second time as an
  unrounded kelvin figure ("29.3 °C · 10 kg · 302.40745815413038 K"). The
  kelvin figure now appears only with the Exact reading precision, rounded

- A machine's stored fluids were listed in kilograms and kelvin whatever the

  player's temperature unit and the mass units of the pipe readings.

## Seen and not changed

- **A natural hydrogen pocket at 1,320 kPa.** At the start of the recording
  a duplicant digs into a sealed pocket above the base holding 1.04 kg of
  hydrogen a tile at 36 °C. With the molecule's mass of 0.1.4 that is
  1,320 kPa, nine times the base's pressure. Within a second it has
  spread down the ladder shaft, and the hydrogen left behind reads −167 to
  −179 °C; the shaft shows as a blue column in the temperature overlay. It
  warms over the next cycles (41 °C by cycle 14) and is mixed into the
  base's air by cycle 23. Energy is conserved; the cold is the work the
  expanding gas did. Two things make it more violent than a player would
  expect. The world's gas pockets keep ONI's mass per tile, which for a
  light gas is a high pressure. And the game's specific heat gives hydrogen
  a heat capacity ratio of 2.72, so a ninefold expansion takes it to about a
  quarter of its absolute temperature where real hydrogen would keep half
  ([material catalogue](material-catalogue.md#hydrogen-expansion-below-1-k-fixed-2026-11-02)).
  Either could be changed (pockets brought to their surroundings' pressure
  when a world is first taken over, or a physical heat capacity for
  hydrogen), and both change what colonies hold, so they are the user's to
  decide. The venting also cost time: the first twenty minutes of the
  session ran with owner ticks of 100 to 150 ms, against about 50 ms
  afterwards.
- **Speed.** With the 3× button the game advanced 2.84 game seconds per
  real second on average over 112 unpaused half-minute windows (1.63 to
  3.15; ten windows below 2.5). The 2× button held 2.00 and the 1× button
  1.00, including the first twenty minutes. No timing sample was dropped
  and the owner never faulted. This is the first measurement of this
  colony on the user's Windows PC; the colony is small (56,000 fluid
  cells), so it says little about the full-colony saves.
- **Short freezes.** 175 frames of the session took longer than 100 ms,
  about one every half minute, each a garbage collection of 0.2 to 0.3
  seconds with a managed heap between 1.7 and 3.5 GB. Each autosave froze
  the game for 1.1 to 1.5 seconds. How much of either is the mod's has not
  been measured against the unmodified game.
- **One garbled picture.** For about 0.2 seconds at 87:30 the whole screen,
  ONI's own panels included, is in false colours. The frames before and
  after are normal. The recording is 10-bit HDR; nothing in the log marks
  that moment and the mod draws none of those panels, so this is taken to
  be a fault of the capture and was not pursued.
- The cold air around the metal refinery (−30 to −78 °C) is the ice biome
  and its wheezeworts.

## Verification

On the Linux game harness, with packages of the working tree:

- `pipe-throughput` on the playtest's pipe code: the pump draws 500.0 kg in
  50 seconds, the first reservoir passes 250.0 kg and the second gains
  228.0 kg (the check fails, as the recording does). With the fix: 500.0 kg
  drawn, 500.0 kg gained, every pipe account exact.
- The two acceptance batches that hold the machine-pipe scenarios pass
  whole on the fixed build: `mixed-pipe-cold`, `mixed-pipe-machines`,
  `mixed-pipe-heat-machines`, `mixed-pipe-retire` and `mixed-pipe-readers`;
  `mixed-pipe-colony`, `mixed-pipe-catalogue`, `pitcher-pump`,
  `storage-ledger` and `pipe-throughput`. Owner mass and energy errors stay
  below 3e-15.
- The 544 Python tests (11 platform skips), the pipe hook tests against the
  game's own assembly, the pipe unit tests, the inspection and description
  text tests and the report tests pass.

Native code is untouched.

On the supplied save, in the Windows player under Proton (600 ticks with 3×
selected, 120 pipe ticks): with the playtest's pipe code the colony's
machines put 361 packets (3,610 kg) into pipes; with the fix 723 packets
(7,230 kg). Both runs restore the save with its owner image reproduced and
close every pipe account; owner ticks average 79 ms in both.

The pushed revision `4bdfceb` passed the quick acceptance tier, 46 of 46
steps (run `quick-v1`): the Linux build with 94 native tests and the Windows
build with 95 under Wine, the 15 Linux scenario batches, the nine base-game
asteroids at 3,000 frames each, a 3,000-frame day on the frozen older save,
and the Windows player under Proton (eight batches, contact, new colony and
save, prepared cycle, autosave cold load, settings save and load, and the
Spaced Out! source, cold and menu loads with their settings checks). Not
tested: Windows itself.
