# First session on the published build, 2026-11-18/19

The user uploaded `4bdfceb` (0.1.8) to the Steam Workshop and played it for
103 minutes on their Windows PC (Ryzen 9 9950X, RTX 5070 Ti, 2560×1440,
500 Hz) with two other mods active (Super Duplicant, No Critter
Overcrowding). The colony is the Spaced Out! save carried since 0.1.4; the
session ran from cycle 23 to cycle 33 with five duplicants. The recording,
`Player.log` and the final save are in `published_playtests` on the share and
stay private; the save is also `local/saves/published-nov19.sav`.

## The final save did not load (fixed, 0.1.9)

Loading the session's last save with the published build refuses the colony:
"World membership cannot abandon an open owner cell". The save holds 360 open
owner cells with 1,090 kg of oxygen at 100 °C along a straight line from
(635, 341) to (601, 0), in the last 35 columns of the 636-column grid, which
belong to no asteroid.

Cause: ONI destroys a meteor only a tenth of the grid beyond its edge and
keeps emitting its exhaust at `Grid.PosToCell` of its position until then. A
meteor that leaves the starting asteroid's left edge writes into the last
columns of the row below, because the cell index wraps. The mod treated gas
that ONI holds in a closed cell as a native solid that had melted there,
opened the cell and took the gas, also outside every world. Play went on
unchanged, the save was written, and the next restore's world membership
check refused it. Any Spaced Out! colony can meet this as soon as a meteor
shower reaches the starting asteroid's left edge.

Fixed ([cells outside every world](colony-owner.md#cells-outside-every-world)):

- The owner never takes fluid ONI holds in a wall outside every world. That
  gas stays ONI's, as without the mod.
- A colony already saved with such cells loads: the restore reproduces the
  saved image, then those cells give up their gas (booked as extracted) and
  close. `Player.log` reports the cells and kilograms released.
- A rocket interior joins the world membership when the game allocates it,
  before its template's gases arrive, so they are still taken.
- The refusal message names the number of cells and where they are.

This changes no solver arithmetic and no checkpoint bytes; the membership mask
gained one value (`world_mask_version` 2). Saves of 0.1.8 load in 0.1.9.

Verification:

- `ma_oni_colony_tests --terrain-order-only`, new group "outside every world":
  a marked open cell restores exactly, gives up its gas and closes, and gas

  pacing). The group fails on the 0.1.8 capture. All 94 native tests and the
  544 Python tests pass.
- The user's save in the Windows player under Proton: refused by `4bdfceb`;
  with the fix it restores at frame 101,524, releases 360 cells and
  1,090.0 kg, and runs 600 ticks with relative mass and energy errors of
  9e-13 and 2e-14 and no fault.
- In the Windows player under Proton, with a package of the working tree:
  `spaced-out-worlds`, `spaced-out-rocket`, `spaced-out-rocket-ports`,
  `spaced-out-input-flush` and `spaced-out-rocket-retire` in one game;
  `spaced-out-rocket`, `spaced-out-rocket-ports`, `spaced-out-cargo-gas`,
  `spaced-out-cargo-liquid`, `spaced-out-rocket-flight` and

## Speed fell to about a third of the previous session

With the 3× button the game advanced 1.34 game seconds a second on average
over 61 half-minute windows (0.56 to 2.48); the previous session held 2.84.
At 1× it fell below 0.95 in 23 of 144 windows and below 0.8 in eight. The
owner tick averaged 124 ms (60 to 207 by window; 69 ms over the first five
minutes, 108 ms over the last fifteen), 83 % of it in the flow stage, against
about 50 ms in the previous session. In 89 of 207 windows the tick's 95th
percentile was longer than the 200 ms of game time it advances.

The colony hardly grew (79,400 open cells at the start, 82,200 at the end).
A game-free replay of the final save
([how](performance-acceptance.md#game-free-speed-check-2026-11-16), capture
`published-nov19`) puts the cost in one region: the surface of the second
asteroid, 7,088 open cells, which takes 21 flow intervals a tick where a quiet
region takes two. The base, 10,048 cells, takes 2.1. On that surface a pool
of water at 24 °C lies open to space at about (369–379, 121–124). The cell
above it holds saturated steam at 3.1 kPa, the next ones a few pascals at
−23 °C with traces of liquid, and the whole sky above holds steam at
0.04 Pa, which ONI deletes from 103 space-exposed cells every tick. In 600
ticks the journal has 1,003 kg of water entering that surface in 327 native
additions. This is the already known cost of gas venting into space
([measurements](performance-acceptance.md#november-18-speed-experiments)),
here fed without end by a liquid boiling into vacuum. What exactly closes
the loop and what would stop it is being studied on the capture; nothing was
changed for it in 0.1.9.

## Debris

During the session the mod handed the game 284,000 solid byproducts, of which
133,000 became new debris items and 151,000 were merged into an item already
in their cell: ice 166,777 pieces (10.0 t), brine ice 44,072 (0.5 t), gold
41,600 (3.4 t), polluted ice 17,226 (1.6 t), solid carbon dioxide 11,653
(0.2 t), igneous rock 515 (0.7 t). More than half the pieces weighed between
1 and 10 g, the size at which a [byproduct batch](colony-owner.md#byproduct-batches)
is released. That is about 21 new items a second. The ice is probably
the vapour above the second asteroid's pool freezing as it expands, the gold
and rock the volcano the recording shows at 905 °C. Not changed in
0.1.9; it is part of the same study.

## Frame timing

For the first 74 minutes the engine's frame times agreed with real time. At
frame 93,232, right after an autosave, the engine began reporting 2.0 ms a
frame while 30.7 ms passed (the fault of 2026-11-07 on this 500 Hz display).
The frame timing fix turned V-Sync off after its two windows of 240 frames,
about 15 seconds at that frame rate, and the clock was right for the rest of
the session. About 11 seconds of game time were lost once (the window ran at
0.74×), and the log's "slowest frame" of 11,109 ms is that missing time put
into one frame, not a freeze on screen. Not changed: the two windows are what
keeps a single slow stretch from switching V-Sync off.

## Seen and not changed

- Each of the 13 saves froze the game for 1.4 to 2.3 seconds (1.1 to 1.5 in
  the previous session); the managed heap ranged from 1.8 to 4.3 GB. Apart
  from saves, 135 frames took longer than 100 ms, the longest 0.76 seconds.
- ONI started in "mod safe mode" ("Mod loading was interrupted on the previous
  boot"). That is ONI's own guard after a start that did not finish, here the
  restart that enabling a newly subscribed mod asks for; the mods loaded.

- The pipe fix of `4bdfceb` holds: every pipe behind the reservoirs and the
  refinery is full in the plumbing overlay, with no alternating packets.
- A steam vent's sealed room stood at 1,010 kPa and 145 to 172 °C, a natural
  gas geyser's pocket at 800 kPa. ONI stops a geyser at about 5 kg of gas a
  tile, which for a light gas is a high pressure: the question of the
  hydrogen pockets of [the previous session](playtest-2026-11-18.md#seen-and-not-changed),
  for the user to decide.
- From 69:51 the hover card stopped updating while the mod's panel beside it
  went on. ONI redraws the hover card only while its window has the focus,
  and that moment is the player's last input; the picture itself is frozen
  from 75:10 to 102:40, most likely a sleeping monitor.
- Readings: values below a thousandth print with an exponent ("3.47E-4 kg",
  "8.79E-6 kPa"); a machine's stored fluids are headed "NATIVE FLUID
  INVENTORY … pressure unavailable"; a liquid-full cell says "No gas phase"
  three ways; the speed button read 1.6× while its tooltip said 1.5×; air at
  905 °C is labelled "Breathable". Listed for a later pass over the texts.
- No exception, error dialog, owner fault or suffocation in the session; all
  13 saves completed.
