# A real duplicant in the owned fluid room

The development option `VerifyLiveBreathing` connects the radius-consumption
boundary to an actual organic duplicant. The source uses the ordinary gas
scene with 20–25 completed frames. After projection is established, it selects
an existing world-provider breather, stops its old navigation path and places
it once inside the sealed three-by-two room. Normal navigation and
`OxygenBreather.Sim200ms` continue. The control neither invokes artificial
breathing ticks nor substitutes a game timestep.

An accepted oxygen request must have the real world-provider callback data
pointing to that exact duplicant and retain its radius-three flag and original
handle. The native receipt must match that request's source epoch, cell,
constituent and available mass. A read-only observer around the provider's void
callback records its actual handle while the original callback executes. The
duplicant's public consumed-gas event must then deliver that same physical mass
and temperature. Previously queued outside callbacks remain outside this
owned receipt chain. No callback is generated by the observer.

Completion requires a drained, positive chain of requests, receipts and actual
callbacks, retained unconsumed species and traces, and the complete fluid U+K
boundary account at its 128-epsilon operand bound. The boundary permits only
the tracked duplicant's objects; other actors still reject. Source placement
does not grant whole-colony ownership or bypass unsupported fluid mutations.

Saving retains the ordinary serialized duplicant and its actual location.
Loading finds the unique organic breather already inside the room, without
teleporting it. Positive time remains held until it binds through its normal
spawn lifecycle. The [accepted-input journal](native-frame-inputs.md) replays
the actual subsequent oxygen requests and CO2 deposits when checking every
physical frame. A loaded control requires positive original breathing
callbacks, not merely successful reader calls.

The short source keeps the saved atmosphere useful for a subsequent breathing
control. Reloads can run longer; oxygen depletion and exhaled CO2 are allowed
to make the completed mixture unbreathable under the configured policy. The
control supplies neither an oxygen refill nor a CO2 scrubber.

Linux and Proton runners accept `--verify-live-breathing`; the Windows
PowerShell runner accepts `-VerifyLiveBreathing` with `-NativeDomain gas` or
`load`. For example, use `--mode native-domain --frames 20 --save-colony
--verify-live-breathing` for a source, then `--mode native-load-domain
--frames 120 --load-save <source.sav> --verify-live-breathing
--resave-before-resume --reload-in-process` for the saved control.

Both managed adapters build and 248 Python cases pass, with four optional or
platform skips. Portable evidence checks reject mismatched callback handles,
mass, temperature, source epochs, radius flags, disease and undeclared species
changes. Callback handles retain all 32 bits; only -1 means no callback.

and readback pass. The fluid boundary also accepts an actual CO2 particle

This establishes no conserved metabolism. Vanilla's oxygen-to-CO2 policy can
credit requested oxygen before its eventual callback; body and food chemistry,
suit/tube providers, fluid-solid body heat, ventilation and complete long-term
survival remain separate work. `CompleteMetabolismVerified` remains false.
